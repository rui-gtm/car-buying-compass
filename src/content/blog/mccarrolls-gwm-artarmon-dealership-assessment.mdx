## 1. Delivery — Poor

The car arrived with two dents, a scratch above the driver's door handle and a paint stain on the
bonnet. The damage was visible immediately and reported on the spot.

[See the condition the car was in at collection](/#collection).

## 2. First repair — Adequate

The dents and bonnet stain were fixed within about three days. The driver's door scratch was not
resolved, and no explanation was given at the time about what further work it might need.

[Read about the first repair](/#dents-fixed).

## 3. Second repair — Poor

The car went back for another three days and was returned with the scratch still visible. To us,
the touch-up paint applied looked worse than before.

[Read about the second collection](/#second-collection).

## 4. Communication — Poor

A sales manager offered two services instead of repairing the door, then said it needed
repainting, an explanation we hadn't heard before, despite two repair visits and repeated
assurances that the damage was fixed.

[Read about the two repair attempts](/#two-attempts).

## 5. Complaint handling — Poor

McCarroll's offer to repaint the door and provide three services only followed notice of an NCAT
claim. The offer has not resolved the dispute, and a hearing is yet to be scheduled.

[Read about the NCAT offer](/#ncat-offer).

## What to take from this

If you are buying new, inspect the car carefully before you accept it, and get any agreed repairs
in writing. The [pre-deposit and delivery checklists](/guides) cover what to look for and how to
escalate a complaint if a dealer does not fix the problem.
