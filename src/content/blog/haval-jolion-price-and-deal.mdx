Price is usually the first question and the last one settled. Before comparing dealers, it helps
to know what the drive-away number should already include, what a reasonable discount looks like
against list price, and which optional extras are worth paying for versus politely declining.

## Drive-away pricing by variant

The table below is a starting point — treat it as the shape of the pricing ladder rather than a
quote, since drive-away figures move with state, run-out timing and current promotions.

| Variant | Powertrain | Typical drive-away from | Notable inclusions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Premium | 1.5T petrol, DCT | Entry price point | Cloth seats, 17-inch alloys, core ADAS suite |
| Lux | 1.5T petrol or Hybrid | Mid-range step-up | Leather-look seats, larger touchscreen, wireless charging |
| Vanta | 1.5T petrol or Hybrid | Top of the range | Two-tone paint, unique styling pack, upgraded audio |

## What a fair discount looks like

Dealers rarely move much off list on a car this recently updated, but drive-away deals do vary by
month, particularly close to end-of-quarter. Get at least two written quotes for the same variant
and colour before treating any single number as final.

## Add-ons worth questioning

Not every line item on the tax invoice adds value. These are the ones to ask about specifically.

<CardGrid columns={3}>
  <Card eyebrow="Often skippable" title="Paint protection">
    Aftermarket paint sealant is frequently marked up well beyond its cost. A ceramic detail from an
    independent detailer is usually better value.
  </Card>
  <Card eyebrow="Ask for the film spec" title="Window tint">
    Reasonable if it is factory-fitted film to a stated legal darkness — get the product name and
    warranty in writing rather than "tint included".
  </Card>
  <Card eyebrow="Compare separately" title="Accessory packs">
    Weather shields, cargo liners and tow bars are often cheaper bought individually after delivery
    than bundled at the point of sale.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Financing and trade-in

<SplitSection imageAlt="Finance paperwork next to a set of car keys" reverse>

Dealer finance can be competitive, but only if you have a second offer to compare it against — a
pre-approval from your bank or a comparison site takes ten minutes and gives you a real number to
negotiate down from.

Trade-in valuations move faster than retail prices. Get an independent online valuation the same
week you visit the dealer, not the month before, and be ready to sell privately if the trade-in
offer sits well below it.
