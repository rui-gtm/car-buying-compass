The Jolion carries a 5-star ANCAP rating dated 2022, applying to both the petrol and hybrid
variants. A star rating is a useful headline, but the category breakdown tells you more about
where that result actually comes from.

## ANCAP category scores

| Category | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Adult occupant protection | Strong |
| Child occupant protection | Strong |
| Vulnerable road user protection | Adequate |
| Safety assist | Strong |

## Standard safety equipment

<CardGrid columns={2}>
  <Card title="Autonomous emergency braking">
    Operates across a wide speed range, including pedestrian and cyclist detection in daylight
    conditions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Adaptive cruise control">
    Maintains a set following distance and works down to stop-and-go traffic on the petrol and
    hybrid variants alike.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lane keep assist">
    Nudges the car back toward the centre of the lane rather than fully overriding steering input.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Blind-spot and rear cross-traffic alert">
    Fitted across the range, with visual warnings in the mirrors and an audible alert when reversing.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Living with the ADAS day to day

<SplitSection imageAlt="Close-up of the lane-keeping warning icon on the instrument cluster">

The systems above are genuinely useful on the highway, where adaptive cruise and lane keeping do
most of the work of long-distance driving. In town, the lane-departure warning can trigger on
narrow or poorly marked suburban streets more often than some drivers want.

Most of the alert sensitivity is adjustable through the settings menu rather than an all-or-nothing
toggle, so it's worth spending five minutes on a test drive finding the level that feels helpful
rather than intrusive before you decide it's a dealbreaker.
