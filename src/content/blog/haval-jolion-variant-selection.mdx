The Jolion range is simple on paper — three trim levels, two drivetrains — but the equipment jump
between grades is worth understanding before you let the finance number decide for you.

## Equipment by grade

| Feature | Premium | Lux | Vanta |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seat trim | Cloth | Leather-look (synthetic) | Leather-look, contrast stitching |
| Touchscreen | Smaller unit | Larger unit, upgraded software | Same as Lux |
| Wireless phone charging | No | Yes | Yes |
| Exterior styling | Standard | Standard | Two-tone roof, unique wheels |
| Driver-assist suite | Core ADAS | Core ADAS | Core ADAS |

Note that the safety suite is consistent across the range — the money above Premium buys comfort
and presentation, not extra crash-avoidance hardware.

## Which grade actually suits you

If the core ADAS suite is identical across the range, the honest question is whether you value the
Lux and Vanta's cabin presentation and equipment enough to pay for it, or whether a Premium with the
drivetrain you actually want is the better use of the same budget.
